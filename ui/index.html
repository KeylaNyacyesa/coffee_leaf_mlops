Dataset: downloaded vs. unique images

Story. The download has ~28k files, but most are duplicates or rotated copies: Healthy has 2,885 files yet only 60 unique images, taken from about 12 distinct leaves. We removed duplicates and split train/test by leaf so the test score is honest. After cleaning, the classes are imbalanced (Miner ≈ 22× Healthy), so training uses class weights and augmentation.
